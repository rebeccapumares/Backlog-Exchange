    Does a market beat how teams usually decide?

    I simulated 30 product teams over 40 sprints. Each member gets a noisy read on every feature's true chance of success. Some are sharp, some are guessing, feature owners are optimistic about their own ideas, and meetings drift toward whatever the manager says first.

    The market price matched an anonymous independent vote. The track-record consensus, which weights each person's stated probability by tokens earned from past accuracy, cut forecast error by 22% against that vote and by 88% against letting the manager decide.

    Final token balances correlated −0.71 with forecasting noise, so the market moves influence toward the people who read users best.
